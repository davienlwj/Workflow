          intervals.icu sync (via Zepp)

          Automatically imports your activities from intervals.icu - runs, rides, swims, walks, hikes, and anything else it reports - each shown with its own sport-appropriate stat (pace for runs, speed for rides, pace/100m for swims), and shows your most recent Resting HR and Sleep as Dashboard stat tiles. Connect your Zepp/Amazfit account directly on a free intervals.icu account (intervals.icu → Settings → Amazfit → sign in with Zepp), then paste your intervals.icu API key below. No equivalent to per-exercise lift data, so a synced weight-training activity comes in as a plain duration/HR entry - workouts with real sets and reps still need to be logged by hand. No backend or paid API needed, just your own free API key - see the app's README.
